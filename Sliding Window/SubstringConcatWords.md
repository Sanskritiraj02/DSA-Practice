# Substring with Concatenation of All Words

🔗 https://leetcode.com/problems/substring-with-concatenation-of-all-words/

---

## 🧠 Approach
- Sliding window in chunks of word length
- Use hashmap to track frequency
- Handle multiple starting offsets

---

## ⚡ Complexity
- Time: O(n * k)
- Space: O(n)

---

## 💡 Key Insight
Slide window in word-sized chunks instead of characters.
