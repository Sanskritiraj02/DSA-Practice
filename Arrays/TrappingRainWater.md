# Trapping Rain Water

🔗 https://leetcode.com/problems/trapping-rain-water/

---

## 🧠 Approach
- Use two pointers
- Track leftMax and rightMax
- Calculate trapped water at each step

---

## ⚡ Complexity
- Time: O(n)
- Space: O(1)

---

## 💡 Key Insight
Water trapped depends on the smaller boundary (min(leftMax, rightMax)).
